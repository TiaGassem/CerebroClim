CerebroClim
Heatwave & Cold-Wave Brain Risk Simulator

A free, open-access tool showing how heat waves, cold waves, ozone and PM2.5 pollution and climate change 2050 affect the blood-brain barrier and M1 microglial neuroinflammation - with a 48-hour prevention window, two-region comparison, alerts and a PDF report. For everyone: older adults, families, clinicians, researchers, schools and workplaces.

What you can do

Past · Present · Future
Copernicus ERA5 archive, 7-day Open-Meteo forecast and a 2050 climate scenario, computed together.
Search or pin any place
Interactive map, single region or side-by-side comparison of two cities or countries.
Prevention in time
48-hour pre-cooling / re-warming window and protocols for seniors, hypertension and metabolic risk.
Pollution included
Ground-level ozone and PM2.5 raise the modelled risk; the 2050 engine adds slower barrier repair.
Alerts & reports
Early-warning e-mail, browser notifications, WhatsApp text and a clinical-style PDF with references.
Learn & share
Lessons on heatwave causes, the ozone layer vs ground-level ozone, and the #HeatSafe48 awareness toolkit.

Who is it for?

  • General public & families: understand your risk and act 48 hours before the peak.
  • Older adults & caregivers: simple prevention steps and check-in ideas.
  • Clinicians & public health: briefing PDF and early-warning e-mail (research tool, not a diagnostic device).
  • Researchers & students: transparent ODE model, assumptions and sources.
  • Workplaces & industry: heat-stress planning and awareness material.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, free to use online, no account needed.

Does it diagnose or give medical advice?

No. It is a phenomenological research and education model that estimates relative risk. Parameters are illustrative and not fitted to patient data. In an emergency (confusion, fainting, very high body temperature) call your local emergency number.

Where does the data come from?

Copernicus ERA5 reanalysis, Open-Meteo weather forecasts and Open-Meteo air-quality data (ozone and PM2.5).

Does it work on my phone?

Yes: Android, iPhone, tablets and PCs, in English, French and Arabic. Use the install steps above.
