This page does not use cookies or trackers of its own and does not collect personal data. GitHub (hosting) may keep standard server logs.

The simulator (hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud)

  • Place names you type are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim (geocoding) and coordinates to Open-Meteo (weather, ERA5 archive, air quality) to compute results.
  • If you use the alert e-mail, the address you type and the alert text are sent to the configured e-mail provider (Resend or an SMTP server) only to deliver that message. The app does not keep a list of e-mail addresses.
  • Results are kept only in your browser session and are discarded when you close or reset the app.
  • Streamlit may process technical logs as the hosting provider.
